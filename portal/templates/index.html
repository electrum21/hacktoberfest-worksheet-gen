A private portal for independent tutors

Make worksheets on your own laptop, preview them as your student will see them, then send each student a private link. Multiple choice is marked instantly. You mark the rest and send back a score.

Create an account Log in

How it works

  1. Generate and check a worksheet in the app on your laptop.
  2. Preview it as the student, then assign it to one or more students.
  3. Students open their link on any phone or computer and submit.
  4. Pull the answers into your laptop app, mark, and send the score back.

Answer keys, mark schemes and each student's weak-topic profile stay on your laptop. This server holds first names, the questions you assign, and what students answer.
